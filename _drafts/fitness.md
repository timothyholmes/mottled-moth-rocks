Over the last few years I documented the process I use to work through my life. The goal throughout the development of that process was increase the amout of time spent doing things that actually leaving me fulfilled, rather than frittering it away on nonesense that offers only fleeting rewards. I needed a framework for **what to do** as much as how to do it.

The tool I used for choosing how to spend my time is a [virtues framework.](/system/2024/08/14/virtues.html) I developed an ontology of seven, helpful lenses for identifying good uses of time over the long haul.

1. [Fitness](/virtues/fitness.html)
2. Creativity
3. Knowledge
4. Network
5. Spirit
6. Home
7. Lore

## Eating Well

Use a calorie app to track food. Tough relationship with food at times, designing environment to work with you.

Helpful tools: food scale, good water bottle of generous size, 32-40oz, Apple Watch, Lose It

A lot of times in my life, my eating habits have left me feeling over-stuffed or poorly fueled. I started monitoring my diet more closely, and it's done a lot for me. My weight is stable, and knowing.

## Exercising

Lifting weights for strength. Growing old strongly. Cardio with something fun, like swimming, biking, or walking.

Include some studies about the benefits of this on all aspects, frame through other virtues?

Bike recommendations. Aluminum frame, older is fine, learn to work on it.
